About Never Land

    It is home of Peter Pan, The Lost Boys, Mermainds and Tinker Bell (Beware of Captain Hook !)
    You can visit Skull Rock, Mermaid Lagoon, Hangman's Tree, and Crocodile Creek
    To reach the island, you must fly second to the right and straight on till morning
    You will see beautiful views, plants and wildlife
